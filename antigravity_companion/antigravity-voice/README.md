# Antigravity Voice Companion

A native, zero-lag voice companion extension for **Antigravity IDE** that reads assistant responses aloud with natural human intonation.

## Features

- 🔊 **Interactive Status Bar**: One-click menu in your bottom status bar to toggle on/off, change voices, and adjust speed.
- ⚡ **4-Layer Progressive Chunking**:
  - **Layer 1 (1 sentence)**: Audio starts within ~1 second.
  - **Layer 2 (2 sentences)**: Plays continuously with zero awkward pause.
  - **Layer 3 (5 sentences)**: Extended buffer retaining natural speech rhythm.
  - **Layer 4 (Remaining text)**: Unified cohesive generation preserving full prosody and sentence transitions.
- 🎙️ **Premium Neural Voices**: Includes natural US and UK voices (Andrew, Jenny, Ava, Brian, Guy, Emma, Ryan, Sonia).
- 🚀 **Zero GPU / VRAM usage**: Cloud-streamed speech leaves your local GPU completely free for coding and compilation.
- ⚙️ **Native IDE Settings**: Full integration into Antigravity Settings (`Ctrl + ,`).
